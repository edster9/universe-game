# Living with the island, stage 6: keeping boars out. Hungry boars go for
# food left lying about, but flee a person, so they raid a camp while its
# owner is away. A raised cache, poles lashed into a platform 2 m up, is
# higher than a boar can climb or jump (1 m), but not a person (2.5 m). See
# docs/challenges/living-with-the-island.md.
world living.toml
as survivor
luck average

include skills/hunt-a-boar.txt
expect said you cut it up: fat
repeat 12
  try drink water
end

# At daylight, poles and fibre for a cache, built by the stream.
wait 6 h
go forest
repeat 6
  gather sticks
end
repeat 2
  gather bushes
end
go stream
assemble raised cache
expect said you put together the raised cache of wood and plant fibre
drop cache
take fat
put fat in cache
take fat
put fat in cache
look
expect said in the raised cache of wood and plant fibre: lump of fat (4.000001 kg), lump of fat (3.999999 kg)
expect said things here:
expect said lump of fat
repeat 12
  try drink water
end

# A night away, asleep on the beach. The fat left on the ground is gone;
# the cache's is not.
go beach
sleep
go forest
go stream
try take fat
expect said the lump of fat is in the raised cache of wood and plant fibre
take fat from cache
expect said you take the lump of fat
expect alive
expect conserved
