# Stranded, stage 1: the right routine keeps a person alive for ten days.
# Drink at the stream twice a day, gather and eat shellfish, throw away the
# shells, and rest. With average luck, searches keep finding shellfish until
# the bed is half gone; after that the body lives on its fat.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

repeat 10
  try go stream        # each day ends at the stream, so this is only needed on day one
  repeat 6
    try drink water
  end
  go beach
  repeat 4
    try gather shellfish
    try eat shellfish
    try drop shell
  end
  wait 8 h
  go stream
  repeat 6
    try drink water
  end
  wait 15 h
end
wait 6 h

expect alive
expect time after 10 day
