# Stranded, stage 7: the raft. Fell six logs, twist a rope, haul the logs to
# the beach two at a time, and lash them together. Buoyancy is the new law:
# wood is lighter than the sea, so the raft floats, and its datasheet says how
# much more it carries: enough for a person. Too heavy to carry, it stays on
# the beach where it was built.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

include skills/cast-an-axe-head.txt

go forest
go stream

  try drink water
end
go forest
repeat 3
  gather trees
  drop largest wood
end
go stream
repeat 6
  try drink water
end
go forest
repeat 3
  gather trees
  drop largest wood
end
gather bushes
work fibre into rope
# Empty hands: two logs are all a person can carry.
drop rope
drop axe
drop flake
drop mould

drop wood

  take largest wood
  take largest wood
  go beach
  expect said (that took 8 min 20 s.)

  drop wood
  go forest
end
take rope
take axe
go beach
assemble raft

expect said buoyancy: floats in the sea, carrying up to 85
try take raft
