# A companion, stage 4: barter, and whose things are whose. The stranger
# values things in their own words (an iron barb above all, then fish, the
# pale flesh they live on, and wood), and trades only when what they'd get
# is worth as much to them as what they'd give. A lump of driftwood won't
# buy their barb; the islander's flint spear will. Ownership lives in
# minds: the shells the stranger drops on the beach are still theirs.
# Taken while they're off at the stream, nothing happens; taken while they
# watch, they remember, and want nothing more to do with the islander. See
# docs/challenges/a-companion.md.
world companion.toml
as survivor
luck average
rules player

go to driftwood
gather driftwood
try offer wood to the stranger for barb
expect said the stranger says, "the iron barb is worth more to me than the lump of wood."
try offer wood to the stranger for fish
expect said the stranger says, "i haven't got fish."
include skills/make-a-spear.txt
go forest
go beach
offer spear to the stranger for barb
expect said the stranger takes the spear of wood and flint and gives you the flake of dark metal
inventory
expect said flake of dark metal (400 g)

# The stranger is off at the stream: a shell taken, unseen.
wait until 13:00
look
expect not said the stranger
go to shell
take shell
expect not said sees you
wait until 15:00
ask the stranger to gather driftwood
expect said the stranger agrees

# Taken while they watch.
take shell
expect said the stranger sees you take it: it's theirs
try ask the stranger to gather driftwood
expect said the stranger says, "you took what's mine."
try offer flake to the stranger for spear
expect said the stranger says, "you took what's mine."
expect conserved
