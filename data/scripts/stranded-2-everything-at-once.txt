# Stranded, stage 2: pile everything on before there's a flame, and it
# still catches: the ember nestles into the finest tinder, and each size
# lights the next. A fire laid first and lit after works, as in life. (Until
# 2026-10-01 this failed, the ember's heat spread too thin; the owner asked
# for it to work. See docs/challenges/skill-grounds.md, step 4.)
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

# Gather fuel in sizes (tufts of grass, twigs, sticks) and five stones, and
# build a fire ring to hold the fire.
go forest
gather sticks
gather sticks
repeat 3
  gather grass
end
repeat 3
  gather twigs
end
go hillside
repeat 5
  gather stones
end
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring

repeat 3
  put grass in ring
end
repeat 3
  put smallest wood in ring
end
rub wood against wood into ring
wait 5 min
look
expect said lump of wood and ash
expect said burning
expect conserved
