# Stranded, stage 8: ways not to cross. Nobody swims 2 km. A raft of driftwood
# floats, but not with a person on it. A single log carries even less. The sea
# isn't something to ride, and a raft is no use on land.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

try go islet
expect said the way to the next island crosses the sea
try go islet on sea
expect said you can't cross on the sea
go forest
gather bushes
work fibre into rope
go beach
repeat 6
  gather driftwood
end
assemble raft
drop raft
try go islet on raft
expect said the raft would sink under you: it carries up to 8.5
try go forest on raft
expect said there's nothing to cross
expect alive
expect conserved
