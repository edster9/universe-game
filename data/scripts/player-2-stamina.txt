# Players' rules: stamina. Vitality brings 200 W; hard work takes 300 W, and
# the difference comes out of a player's stamina, 3 MJ when full: about a
# working day. A day and more of hauling driftwood uses it up, and the player
# works at half pace until they've rested. See docs/ideas/game-interface.md.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average
rules player

# Hauling, and setting it down, as the driftwood thins.
repeat 36
  try gather driftwood
  try drop wood
end
datasheet me
expect said stamina: 0
# Out of stamina, even walking goes at half pace: 8 min 20 s to the forest,
# not 4 min 10 s.
go forest
expect said that took 8 min 20 s
go beach
wait 8 h
datasheet me
expect not said stamina: 0
go forest
expect said that took 4 min 10 s
expect alive
expect conserved
