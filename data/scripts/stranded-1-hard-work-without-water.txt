# Stranded, stage 1: working hard in the heat without drinking kills in about
# half a day, far sooner than resting does. Sweat keeps the body cool, but it
# costs water.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

repeat 200
  try gather shellfish
  try eat shellfish
  try drop shell
end
expect dead of thirst
expect time before 1 day
