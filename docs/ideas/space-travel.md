# Space, gravity, and faster than light

Recorded 2026-09-29 **as a placeholder for a discussion the owner has asked for**. Nothing here is decided. The requirement is "Vitality, and moving to real time and 3D" in [requirements.md](../requirements.md).

## The owner's starting points

- Space and gravity are very important. The climb ends with a spaceship built, launched, and put into orbit ([production.md](production.md)), under real laws.
- Past that, real physics is too slow for a game. By Newtonian flight, the Moon is days away (Apollo took about three days each way), and other planets months or years. So the universe will need science-fiction laws: hyperdrive, light speed, perhaps wormholes, invented with rules we apply, as "theoretical laws".

## What's already decided that bears on it

- **Time is negotiable; matter and energy are not** ([game-interface.md](game-interface.md)). A faster-than-light law could keep that: it may shorten a journey, but should still cost energy and matter, from named sources.
- **Universes can differ in their laws** ([universes.md](universes.md)).
- **Laws, not things.** A hyperdrive would be a law (what it needs, what it does), and drives would be things in data, built like anything else.

## Questions for the discussion

- Which invented laws, and what do they cost: energy, a rare material, a place (a gate, a wormhole's mouth), a risk?
- Where does real physics stop and invention start: orbit and moons by Newton, other systems by invention?
- How is space laid out for a small, networked game where players should meet each other?
