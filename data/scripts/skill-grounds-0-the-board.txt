# The skill grounds (docs/challenges/skill-grounds.md), step 3: the board.
# One open place: from the clearing in the middle, six zones in a ring, each
# with everything its own skills need within reach of its middle, so
# nothing is fetched from another zone. Each zone's kit is gathered and used
# here, walking to it from the clearing.
world skill-grounds.toml
as player
luck average

look
expect said the skill grounds
expect said the hearth
expect said the forge
expect said the knapping ground
expect said the fibre grove
expect said the stream bank
expect said the woodland
expect said bog iron in a boggy hollow
expect said fibrous bushes
expect said mussels in the shallows
expect said standing trees
expect not said a boar

# The knapping ground: a spear, from its own flint, stones, and sticks.
go to the knapping ground
expect said that took 2
go to flint
gather flint
go to stones
gather stones
go to sticks
gather sticks
work flint into flake with stone
work stick into shaft with flake
assemble spear
expect said spear
drop stone

# The hearth: a fire, fed by sizes, each when the last has caught.
walk to 0 0
go to the hearth
go to grass
gather grass x3
go to twigs
gather twigs x4
expect said you find 20 g of wood, a wood twig
go to sticks
gather sticks x6
expect said a wood stick
go to deadwood
gather deadwood

go to stones

  put twig in ring

  put stick in ring
