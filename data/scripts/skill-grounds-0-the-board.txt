# The skill grounds (docs/challenges/skill-grounds.md), step 3: the board.
# From the clearing, every ground is about half a minute's walk, and has
# what its skills need within reach of its middle. A fire at the hearth,
# fed tinder, twigs, sticks, and at last a log, by the words for sizes.
world skill-grounds.toml
as player
luck average

look
expect said the clearing
expect said the woodland, the knapping ground, the fibre grove, the stream bank, the hearth

go woodland
expect said that took 25 s
look
expect said standing trees
expect said a boar
go knapping
look
expect said flint nodules
expect said loose stones
expect said fallen sticks
go clearing
go grove
look
expect said fibrous bushes
gather bushes
expect said plant fibre
go stream
look
expect said clay in the bank
expect said bog iron in the streambed
expect said mussels in the shallows
gather mussels
drop all
go hearth
look
expect said dry grass
expect said dry twigs
expect said deadwood
expect time before 5 min

# A fire, fed by sizes, each when the last has caught.
gather grass x3
gather twigs x4
expect said you find 20 g of wood, a wood twig
gather sticks x6
expect said a wood stick
gather deadwood
expect said a wood log
gather stones x5
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring
put grass in ring
rub wood against wood into ring
wait until grass is burning
expect said burning
put grass in ring
repeat 4
  put twig in ring
end
wait until twig is burning
expect said the twig is burning
put stick in ring
put stick in ring
wait until stick is burning
expect said the stick is burning
repeat 3
  put stick in ring
end
wait 1 min
put log in ring
wait until log is burning
expect said the log is burning
expect alive
expect conserved
