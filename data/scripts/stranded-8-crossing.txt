# Stranded, stage 8: the crossing, in perfect conditions. The next island is
# 2 km across a calm sea. There's no walking there; only something that floats
# and carries you can take you. Propulsion against drag gives the speed: a
# paddle pushes 30% of the effort into the water, bare hands only 5%, so the
# raft crosses in an hour with a paddle and nearly two without.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

include skills/build-a-raft.txt
try go islet
expect said you need something that floats
gather driftwood
work wood into paddle with axe
datasheet paddle
expect said pushes with: 30% of the effort
go stream
repeat 6
  try drink water
end
go beach
go islet on raft
expect said you cross to the next island on the raft. (that took 1 h.)
look
expect said raft
drop paddle
go beach on raft
expect said (that took 1 h 49 min.)
expect alive
expect conserved
