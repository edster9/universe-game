# Where am I?, stage 1: night. The sun sets at 18:00, the air cools toward
# 292 K at midnight, and it's too dark to search for anything. A body shivers
# to stay warm. A fire gives light to search by, and rubbing sticks at night
# works as by day: you rub until the tinder catches.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

# By day: fuel in sizes, and stones for a ring.
go forest
gather sticks
repeat 3
  gather grass
end
repeat 3
  gather twigs
end
go hillside
repeat 5
  gather stones
end
assemble fire ring
go forest
go beach
wait 10 h
look
expect said night, and dark
try gather driftwood
expect said too dark to search
datasheet survivor
expect said temperature: 310 k
# Make fire in the dark.
drop fire ring
put grass in ring
rub wood against wood into ring
expect said smouldering
wait 10
put grass in ring
put grass in ring
wait 10
repeat 3
  put smallest wood in ring
end
wait 30
put wood in ring
wait 1 min
look
expect said night, lit by fire
gather driftwood
expect said you find 2 kg of wood
expect alive
expect conserved
