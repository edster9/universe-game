# Skill: hunt a boar, on the living island, from the beach. By day, gather
# what a fire needs and make a honed spear; at night, spear a sleeping boar
# in the forest, and find it dead by the stream. Butcher it there with the
# spear. Ends by the stream, carrying the spear, a fire ring, and the fire's
# makings, with the cuts on the ground.
go forest
repeat 4
  gather sticks
end
repeat 6
  gather grass
end
repeat 4
  gather twigs
end
go hillside
repeat 6
  gather stones
end
gather flint
work flint into flake with stone
rub flake against stone for 30 min
work largest wood into shaft with flake
assemble spear
assemble fire ring
go forest
go stream
repeat 6
  try drink water
end
go beach
wait 12 h
go forest
attack boar with spear
wait 1 h
go stream
butcher boar with spear
