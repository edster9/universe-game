# Living with the island, stage 3: a sharper edge wounds worse. Honing the
# flint flake on a stone for half an hour takes its edge from 5 mm to 2 mm,
# so the spear wounds a sleeping boar at 10 g a second: more than it can
# lose. The boar still gores the islander and runs, and dies of bleeding by
# the stream, where it lies.
world living.toml
as survivor
luck average

go forest
gather sticks
go hillside
gather stones
gather flint
work flint into flake with stone
rub flake against stone for 30 min
work wood into shaft with flake
assemble spear
datasheet spear
expect said edge width: 2 mm
go forest
go stream
repeat 6
  try drink water
end
go beach
wait 13 h
go forest
attack boar with spear
expect said it's bleeding 10 g a second
expect said a boar goes for you, and wounds you
wait 1 h
go stream
look
expect said k, dead)
datasheet boar
expect said died of: bleeding
expect alive
expect conserved
