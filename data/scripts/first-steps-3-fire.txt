# First steps, stage 3: a fire, as the islander on the companion's island,
# by players' rules. From the beach, walk to the forest for fuel in sizes,
# to the hillside for stones, back to the beach, and rub up a fire in a
# ring, the way the stranded challenge proved. Played in the client, it's
# drawn burning. See docs/challenges/first-steps.md.
world companion.toml
as survivor
luck average
rules player

go forest
go to sticks; gather sticks x2
go to grass; gather grass x3
go to twigs; gather twigs x3
go hillside
go to stones; gather stones x5
assemble fire ring
go forest
go beach
drop fire ring

# Light it after sunset, to see the beach by it in the dark.
wait until 18:05

put grass in ring
# Rub until the tuft catches: rubbing stops by itself when it does.
rub wood against wood into ring
wait 10
look
expect said burning
put grass in ring
put grass in ring
wait 10
repeat 3
  put smallest wood in ring
end
wait 30
put wood in ring
put wood in ring
wait 10 min
look
expect said lump of wood and ash
expect said burning
expect said night, lit by fire
expect alive
expect conserved
