# The skill grounds (docs/challenges/skill-grounds.md), step 3: the board.
# One open place: from the clearing in the middle, six zones in a ring, each
# with everything its own skills need within reach of its middle, so
# nothing is fetched from another zone. Each zone's kit is gathered and used
# here, walking to it from the clearing.
world skill-grounds.toml
as player
luck average

look
expect said the skill grounds
expect said the hearth
expect said the forge
expect said the knapping ground
expect said the fibre grove
expect said the stream bank
expect said the woodland
expect said bog iron in a boggy hollow
expect said fibrous bushes
expect said mussels in the shallows
expect said standing trees
expect not said a boar

# The knapping ground: a spear, from its own flint, stones, and sticks.
go to the knapping ground
expect said that took 21 s
gather flint
gather stones
gather sticks
work flint into flake with stone
work stick into shaft with flake
assemble spear
expect said spear
drop stone

# The fibre grove: rope, from its own bushes, and poles.
walk to 0 0
go to the fibre grove
gather bushes x2
gather sticks
gather deadwood
work fibre into rope
expect said rope
drop all

# The forge: everything for a furnace and its fire, all in reach. (The
# islanders don't know a furnace yet: how they come to know metalworking is
# step 5's question.)
walk to 0 0
go to the forge
gather bog iron
gather clay x2
gather stones x6
gather flint
gather grass
gather twigs
gather sticks
gather deadwood
expect said a wood log
drop all

# The stream bank: water, mussels, and roots and nuts.
walk to 0 0
go to the stream bank
gather mussels
gather roots and nuts
try gather fish
gather flint
gather stones
gather sticks
drop all
# Bushes grow in two zones: the nearest is meant, the fibre grove's.
go to bushes
expect said that took 1
walk to 0 0

# The woodland: its trees need an axe, and the boars are in the deep woods.
walk to 0 0
go to the woodland
# (An axe, to the islanders, who don't know one yet: "a part put together".)
try gather trees
expect said you need a part put together for the standing trees
gather flint
gather stones
gather sticks
gather grass
gather twigs
gather bushes
drop all

# The hearth: a fire, fed by sizes, each when the last has caught.
walk to 0 0
go to the hearth
gather grass x3
gather twigs x4
expect said you find 20 g of wood, a wood twig
gather sticks x6
expect said a wood stick
gather deadwood
expect said a wood log
gather stones x5
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring
put grass in ring
rub wood against wood into ring
wait until grass is burning
expect said burning
put grass in ring
repeat 4
  put twig in ring
end
wait until twig is burning
expect said the twig is burning
put stick in ring
put stick in ring
wait until stick is burning
expect said the stick is burning
repeat 3
  put stick in ring
end
wait 1 min
put log in ring
wait until log is burning
expect said the log is burning
expect alive
expect conserved
