# Stranded, stage 2: rubbing two stones only makes warm stone dust. Stone
# doesn't burn, and its dust never gets hot enough to light even a pinch of
# grass.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

# Gather fuel from the forest and five stones from the hillside, and build a
# fire ring to hold the fire.
go forest
gather sticks
gather sticks
gather grass
gather grass
repeat 3
  gather twigs
end
go hillside
repeat 5
  gather stones
end
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring

divide grass
divide grass
divide grass
repeat 3
  put smallest grass in ring
end
gather stones
gather stones
rub stone against stone into ring for 1 min
wait 1 min
look
expect not said burning
expect conserved
