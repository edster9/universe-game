# Stranded, stage 2: fire from two sticks, built the way people really do it.
# Rubbing wears dust off one stick and friction heats it until it smoulders.
# Pinches of grass packed around it catch; their flames light a handful of
# grass, that lights the twigs, and the twigs light the sticks. Nothing in the
# engine says "tinder before kindling": it comes from size, surface, and heat.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

# Gather fuel from the forest and five stones from the hillside, and build a
# fire ring to hold the fire.
go forest
gather sticks
gather sticks
gather grass
gather grass
repeat 3
  gather twigs
end
go hillside
repeat 5
  gather stones
end
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring

# Pull a handful of grass apart until there are pinches, and pack them in.
divide grass
divide grass
divide grass
repeat 3
  put smallest grass in ring
end
rub wood against wood into ring for 1 min
look
expect said burning
wait 10
put largest grass in ring
wait 10
repeat 3
  put smallest wood in ring
end
wait 30
put wood in ring
put wood in ring
wait 10 min
look
expect said lump of wood and ash
expect said burning
expect alive
expect conserved
