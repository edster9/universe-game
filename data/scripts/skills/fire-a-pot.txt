# Skill: make a clay pot and fire it hard in a furnace, starting on the beach
# and knowing the way around. Gather everything first, shape the pot with a
# stone, build a fire ring and a furnace on the hillside, make fire, and light
# the furnace from it. The pot fires at 900 K as the furnace heats, then
# cools for two hours. Ends on the hillside holding the fired pot.
go forest
repeat 3
  gather sticks
end
repeat 3
  gather grass
end
repeat 3
  gather twigs
end
go beach
repeat 3
  gather driftwood
end
go stream
repeat 3
  gather clay
end
go forest
go hillside
repeat 12
  gather stones
end
work smallest clay into pot with stone
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring
assemble furnace
drop furnace
put grass in ring
rub wood against wood into ring
wait 10
put grass in ring
put grass in ring
wait 10
repeat 3
  put smallest wood in ring
end
wait 30
put wood in ring
put pot in furnace
put largest wood in furnace
put largest wood in furnace
light furnace
wait 5 min
datasheet pot
expect said fired clay pot
wait 2 h
take pot from furnace
