# Players' rules: the crossing, as a player. The same raft, built the same
# way (build-a-raft.txt, whose night's sleep is a player's rest), with no
# need to eat or drink; but two days of felling and hauling use up a
# player's stamina, and paddling tired takes twice as long. Rest first, and
# the crossing takes the hour it takes anyone.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average
rules player

include skills/build-a-raft.txt
gather driftwood
work wood into paddle with axe
datasheet me
expect said stamina: 0
sleep for 10 h
expect said you rest until
datasheet me
expect not said stamina: 0
go islet on raft
expect said you cross to the next island on the raft. (that took 1 h.)
expect alive
expect conserved
