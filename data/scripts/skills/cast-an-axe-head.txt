# Skill: cast an iron axe head, starting on the beach of the stranded island
# with nothing. Gather everything first, make fire, light a furnace from it,
# smelt bog iron, and cast the head in a clay mould fired in the same
# furnace. Ends holding an unhoned axe head with a 5 mm edge, a flint flake,
# and a spare stick.
# Gather everything first.
go forest
repeat 3
  gather sticks
end
repeat 3
  gather grass
end
repeat 3
  gather twigs
end
go beach
repeat 3
  gather driftwood
end
go stream
repeat 3
  gather clay
end
gather bog
go forest
go hillside
repeat 12
  gather stones
end
gather flint
work flint into flake with stone
work smallest clay into axe-head mould with flake
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring
assemble furnace
drop furnace
# Make fire, and feed it up to a log.
put grass in ring
rub wood against wood into ring
wait 30
put grass in ring
put grass in ring
wait 10
repeat 3
  put smallest wood in ring
end
wait 30
put wood in ring
# Light the furnace from the fire. The mould fires hard as the furnace heats.
put mould in furnace
put iron in furnace
put largest wood in furnace
put largest wood in furnace
light furnace
wait 3 min
datasheet mould
expect said fired clay axe-head mould
wait 4 min
look
expect said molten iron
pour molten iron into mould
wait 2 h
look
take mould from furnace
take axe head from mould
datasheet axe head
expect said edge width: 5 mm
