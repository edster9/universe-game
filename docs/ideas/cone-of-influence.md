# The cone of influence

Claude's take, 2026-09-30. **Decided the same day**, with the owner's refinement: see the end. The owner's words are in "The cone of influence" in [requirements.md](../requirements.md): if a tree falls and nobody hears it, does it matter? History is written when it's discovered; the measurement reveals the location. This refines "bigger steps where nothing fast is happening" in [the-road-ahead.md](the-road-ahead.md).

## The idea, and one distinction it needs

Agreed: the world only has to be exact where someone could know the difference. Everywhere else it can be vague, and made exact when someone arrives. But "not worked out" covers two different things, and the difference decides whether the world stays honest:

- **Undecided: details nobody could know, and nothing depends on.** Which of its haunts a boar is in right now. Exactly where in the forest a fallen branch lies. These can stay truly undecided until someone looks, and then be decided by chance, from the world's seed. This is the owner's quantum picture.
- **Not yet worked out: things that matter even unwatched.** The boar is getting hungry, the fish on the drying rack is spoiling, the grass is regrowing, and the village smith is working through the day. Nobody needs to watch, but when someone arrives, it must be as if it all happened: the same laws, run over the time that passed, in big steps. History is written when it's discovered, but it has to be the history that *would have* happened.

**The engine already does the first kind.** A flint slope isn't a thousand simulated pebbles: it's a mass, and a search decides by chance, when you look, whether you find a piece. The shellfish bed is a mass that grows. The cone of influence takes that habit from sources to creatures and whole places.

## What the cone is

**Measurement is perception.** That matches "no oracles" exactly: the world must be exact where someone perceives it, *to the detail they perceive*. From the summit, you see the village's smoke, not its people, so the village can stay vague while you stand up there.

**Influence travels no faster than whatever carries it**: a walker, a thrown spear, a sound, sight across the water. So a player's cone is everything they perceive now, plus everything they could reach before the next big step. Walking at 5 km/h, a one-hour step means everything within 5 km of path. Later, a ship's cone is far larger, and a message's larger still: the idea scales to space, where it's the real thing, the light cone.

**Who casts cones? Players** (and, as decided below, NPCs with free minds). If every NPC cast one, the whole world would be exact, and nothing would be saved. NPCs among themselves, far from any player, still live by the laws, in big steps: the village's economy runs whether or not anyone's there.

## Three levels of detail

| Level | Where | How it runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Exact** | Inside a player's cone | Every step, as now |
| **Coarse** | Outside every cone, where things are happening: a village at work, a herd | The same laws in big steps, say an hour. A herd is somewhere in its range, undecided. A day's fishing is a day's catch at the rate the laws give. |
| **Asleep** | Nothing's happening: an empty valley, a stone in the sun | Not run at all. Caught up in one go when a cone reaches it: cooling, spoiling, and growing each have a simple answer for any stretch of time. |

When a cone reaches a place: catch it up to now, decide what was undecided (are the boars here?), and run it exactly from then on.

## What must not bend

1. **Conservation is exact at every level.** An undecided boar's mass is still in the world, somewhere in its range; its hunger is still paid for. The gate checks coarse steps like any other.
2. **No rerolls.** What's revealed depends on the seed and the time, not on when you look or how often. Stepping out of a clearing and back in doesn't reshuffle the boars.
3. **What you saw stays true** unless the laws changed it. Memory already handles corrections.
4. **Dying is an outcome, even unwatched.** A coarse hour can hold a raid on an unwatched cache, decided as a chance over the hour with the odds the fine steps would give.

## The risk, and how we'd guard it

Big steps drift from small ones. The research found this in X4, where players report stations fighting better when nobody's watching ([research/npcs-and-real-time.md](../research/npcs-and-real-time.md)). Our guard: **proofs that run the same world watched and unwatched**. Conserved totals must match exactly; outcomes (the herd alive, the cache raided or not, the smith's day's work) must match within the trials' tolerances. None of the games surveyed described checking this. Our gate makes it natural.

## How it changes the road

It becomes part of step 1, "room to grow", after the two fixes needed regardless (the gate checking changes rather than the whole world, and memory that doesn't keep everything):

- **1a.** The gate and memory fixes.
- **1b.** Places keep their own time: each is exact, coarse, or asleep, depending on whether it's in a player's cone.
- **The proof:** the benchmark (a village's worth of bodies for 30 days, fast, memory flat), plus watched and unwatched agreeing.

## Decided, 2026-09-30

The owner: "go with Claude's best recommendations", with a refinement and a caution ("Deciding it: a balance we'll discover" in [requirements.md](../requirements.md)).

- **Undecided vs not yet worked out**, as above: only what nobody could know stays truly undecided; everything that matters unwatched is caught up by the same laws.
- **Cones are cast by players, and by NPCs whose choices can matter**: the owner's example is an NPC who decides to blow up a planet. In the terms of [npc-minds.md](npc-minds.md), **free minds cast cones; confined and resident ones don't.** A player's body while they're away keeps a small cone of its own.
- **Three levels of detail**, exact, coarse, and asleep, with the four rules that don't bend.
- **Part of step 1** of [the-road-ahead.md](the-road-ahead.md), after the gate and memory fixes.
- **The balance is found by measuring, not guessed.** How big a cone is, how long a coarse step is, and what a server must remember will be tuned as the village grows into several villages with players in different places. The load rises and falls with where players and free minds are. **The measure of success** is how many players can live comfortably in one world with the server keeping up, so the benchmark proofs report that, and we watch it stage by stage.
