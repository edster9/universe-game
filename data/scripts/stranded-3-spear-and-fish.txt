# Stranded, stage 3: fish can't be caught by hand. Make a spear, and a search
# of the shallows finds a fish six times in ten while they're plentiful. With
# average luck, that's every time.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

try gather fish
expect said need a spear
# Make a spear: a stone knocks a flake off flint, the flake shapes a stick
# into a shaft, and flake and shaft go together.
go forest
gather sticks
go hillside
gather stones
gather flint
work flint into flake with stone
work wood into shaft with flake
assemble spear
go forest
go beach
repeat 3
  gather fish
  expect said you find 500 g of fish
  eat fish
end
expect alive
expect conserved
