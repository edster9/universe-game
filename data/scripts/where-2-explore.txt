# Where am I?, stage 2: an unknown island. The castaway wakes knowing no way
# off the beach. Exploring takes half an hour a search and, with average
# luck, finds the nearest way not yet known: the forest, then the stream.
# Walking a way teaches the way back. Searching needs light.
world where-am-i.toml
as survivor
luck average

look
expect said ways out you know: none
try go stream
expect said you don't know a way to stream from here
explore
expect said you find a way to the forest. (that took 30 min.)
explore
expect said you find a way to the stream
go stream
look
expect said ways out you know: the beach
repeat 6
  try drink water
end
explore
expect said you find a way to the forest
go forest
explore
expect said you find a way to the beach
explore
expect said you find a way to the hillside
go hillside
explore
expect said you search around, but find no new way out
go forest
go beach
look
expect said ways out you know: the stream, the forest
# Night: too dark to search.
wait 9 h
try explore
expect said too dark to search
expect alive
expect conserved
