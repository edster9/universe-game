# A stranger's words, stage 2: inventing and naming. The stranger joins their
# iron barb to a stick: nothing they know looks like it, so they name it. Its
# name brings the way to make another. What others make is seen in the words
# each already has, near or exact, and the same word for two things that
# look different makes the engine ask which. See
# docs/challenges/strangers-words.md.
world strangers-words.toml
as stranger
luck average

# Something new, named, with the way it was made.
go forest
gather sticks
join barb and wood
expect said you've made something new: iron barb joined to lump of wood
expect said what do you call it?
call it a stabber
expect said "make a stabber" will make another
inventory
expect said stabber of iron and wood
try make a stabber
expect said you need to be carrying a barb
go hillside
drop the stabber

# To the islander, it's a chip of dark metal on a stick of wood.
become islander
go forest
go hillside
look
expect said flake of dark metal joined to wood stick

# The islander's spear, before anyone names it: like the stranger's stabber.
become islander
include skills/make-a-spear.txt
drop the spear
become stranger
look
expect said something like a stabber, made of glassy grey stone and wood

# Told it's a spear, the stranger has a closer word for it.
become islander
tell the stranger that the spear is a spear
become stranger
look
expect said spear of glassy grey stone and wood
expect said stabber of iron and wood

# The islander takes the barb from the stabber and makes a spear with it.
become islander
disassemble the flake of dark metal joined to wood stick
take the flake
expect said you take the flake of dark metal
go forest
gather sticks
go hillside
work wood into shaft with stone
assemble spear
expect said you put together the spear of dark metal and wood
take the spear of flint and wood

# Two spears in hand, that look different: which?
try drop the spear
expect said which spear: the spear of flint and wood, or the spear of dark metal and wood?
drop the metal one
expect said you drop the spear of dark metal and wood

# To the stranger, the metal-headed spear is a spear, but of iron.
become stranger
look
expect said spear of iron and wood
