# Stranded, stage 7: the wrong raft. Six pieces of driftwood float, but carry
# only 8.5 kg more, far too little for a person. A rope too thin to hold even
# them won't lash them together. And bog iron sinks.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

go forest
gather bushes
repeat 5
  divide smallest fibre
end
work smallest fibre into rope
go beach
repeat 6
  gather driftwood
end
try assemble raft
expect said won't hold it together: it holds up to 7.9
work largest fibre into rope
assemble raft
datasheet raft
expect said buoyancy: floats in the sea, carrying up to 8.5
go stream
gather bog
datasheet iron
expect said buoyancy: sinks in the stream's water
expect conserved
