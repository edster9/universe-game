# Stranded, stage 3: with the worst luck, every search of the shallows comes
# up empty, spear or not. The time is still spent.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck bad

# Make a spear: a stone knocks a flake off flint, the flake shapes a stick
# into a shaft, and flake and shaft go together.
go forest
gather sticks
go hillside
gather stones
gather flint
work flint into flake with stone
work wood into shaft with flake
assemble spear
go forest
go beach
repeat 4
  gather fish
  expect said find nothing
end
expect time after 1 h
expect conserved
