# Stranded, stage 1: working hard in the heat without drinking kills in about
# a day and a half, far sooner than resting does. Sweat keeps the body cool,
# but it costs water. A day of gathering, until it's too dark to search,
# leaves the body little more than a kilogram of water from death.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

repeat 200
  try gather shellfish
  try eat shellfish
  try drop shell
end
try gather shellfish
expect said too dark to search
repeat 48
  wait 30 min
end
expect dead of thirst
expect time before 36 h
