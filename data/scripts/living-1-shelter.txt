# Living with the island, stage 1: shelter. Six sticks and four lumps of
# plant fibre make a lean-to that keeps in 60% of a sleeper's body heat.
# Slept in on the beach, the night costs only what a body burns at rest; it
# has to be put down first, and not everything gives shelter.
world living.toml
as survivor
luck average

go forest
repeat 6
  gather sticks
end
repeat 4
  gather bushes
end
go beach
assemble lean-to
datasheet lean-to
expect said keeps in: 60% of a sleeper's heat
try sleep in lean-to
expect said put the lean-to down first
drop lean-to
go forest
gather sticks
go beach
drop wood
try sleep in lump of wood
expect said gives no shelter
wait 8 h
sleep in lean-to for 10 h
expect said you sleep in the lean-to of wood and plant fibre, and wake at 04:51
datasheet survivor
expect said temperature: 310 k
expect alive
expect conserved
