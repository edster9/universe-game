# A stranger's words, stage 1: seeing and saying in your own words. The
# islander makes a spear; the stranger, whose people have never seen flint
# or a spear, sees what anyone would see, and can't ask for it by a name they
# don't know. Told its name, they know it, and know the next one they see.
# See docs/challenges/strangers-words.md.
world strangers-words.toml
as stranger
luck average

# The stranger's own things, in their own words, and a creature they've
# never seen, by how it looks.
inventory
expect said iron barb
look
expect said a large bristly beast
expect said lump of glassy grey stone
expect not said flint

# The islander makes a spear on the hillside and puts it down.
become islander
include skills/make-a-spear.txt
expect said spear of flint and wood
drop spear
look
expect said flint nodules

# To the stranger it's a barb of a strange stone on a pole.
become stranger
look
expect said barb of glassy grey stone joined to long straight pole of wood
expect not said spear
try take the spear
expect said you don't see the spear here
take the pole
expect said you take the barb of glassy grey stone joined to long straight pole of wood
drop the pole

# The islander tells them what it is, and what the creature is.
become islander
tell the stranger that the spear is a spear
expect said you tell the stranger it's a spear
become stranger
look
expect said spear of glassy grey stone and wood
take the spear
expect said you take the spear of glassy grey stone and wood
drop the spear

# A second spear, which the stranger has never been shown: they know it.
become islander
include skills/make-a-spear.txt
drop the spear
become stranger
look
expect said spear of glassy grey stone and wood (700 g), spear of glassy grey stone and wood (700 g)
