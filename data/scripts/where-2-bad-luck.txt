# Where am I?, stage 2: the worst luck. Every search finds nothing, the
# castaway never finds the stream, the sea can't be drunk, and they die of
# thirst on the beach within four days. Dying is an outcome, not a failure.
world where-am-i.toml
as survivor
luck bad

explore
expect said you search around, but find no new way out
repeat 4
  repeat 20
    try explore
    try go stream
    try drink water
  end
  try drink sea
  try sleep
  wait 4 h
end
expect dead of thirst
expect time before 4 day
