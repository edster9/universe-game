# Stranded, stage 2: an ember against a stick does nothing. The stick barely
# warms.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

# Gather fuel in sizes (tufts of grass, twigs, sticks) and five stones, and
# build a fire ring to hold the fire.
go forest
gather sticks
gather sticks
repeat 3
  gather grass
end
repeat 3
  gather twigs
end
go hillside
repeat 5
  gather stones
end
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring

put largest wood in ring
rub wood against wood into ring for 1 min
wait 5 min
look
expect not said burning
expect conserved
