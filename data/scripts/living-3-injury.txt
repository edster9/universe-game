# Living with the island, stage 3: injury. A wound bleeds, fast at first,
# then half as fast every ten minutes as it clots; bleeding drains the
# body's fluid, and below its minimum a body dies of bleeding. How bad a
# wound is depends on the edge: a crude flint spear (5 mm) wounds a boar at
# 4 g a second, not enough to kill it. Hurt, the boar turns and gores the
# islander with its tusks, then runs. The islander, wounded too, drinks and
# lives.
world living.toml
as survivor
luck average

include skills/make-a-spear.txt
go forest
go stream
repeat 6
  try drink water
end
go beach
wait 14 h
go stream
look
expect said also here: a boar
try attack boar
expect said you have nothing with an edge to wound with
attack boar with spear
expect said you strike a boar with the spear, and wound it: it's bleeding 4 g a second
# The boar turns on the islander in the moment after.
wait 1 min
expect said a boar goes for you, and wounds you: you're bleeding 4 g a second
datasheet survivor
expect said bleeding: 4 g a second
wait 3 h
datasheet survivor
expect not said bleeding
repeat 12
  try drink water
end
expect alive
expect conserved
