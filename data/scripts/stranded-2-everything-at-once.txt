# Stranded, stage 2: pile everything on before there's a flame, and the
# ember's heat is spread too thin to light anything.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

# Gather fuel in sizes (tufts of grass, twigs, sticks) and five stones, and
# build a fire ring to hold the fire.
go forest
gather sticks
gather sticks
repeat 3
  gather grass
end
repeat 3
  gather twigs
end
go hillside
repeat 5
  gather stones
end
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring

repeat 3
  put grass in ring
end
repeat 3
  put smallest wood in ring
end
rub wood against wood into ring for 1 min
wait 5 min
look
expect said lump of wood (20 g
expect not said lump of wood and ash
expect conserved
