# Laws

The register of every law in the engine, started 2026-09-28. A law is stated generally, never in terms of a particular thing. Each lists where it's used. A law used by only one scenario is suspect: it may be a prop for one story rather than a law. See [ideas/world-engine.md](ideas/world-engine.md).

Status: **in** (in the engine), **planned** (needed by an accepted challenge).
