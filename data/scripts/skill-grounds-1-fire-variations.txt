# The skill grounds, step 4: fire done properly (docs/challenges/skill-grounds.md).
# The ways a fire is really made all work, and the ones that fail, fail for
# the reasons they would in life:
# - laid first, tinder to sticks, and lit after: it catches;
# - all fed at once, without waiting: it still climbs to the sticks;
# - an open fire on bare ground: it works, but takes longer to catch;
# - an ember carried in one ring and tipped into tinder in another: a whole
#   tuft is too much for it, tinder teased fine catches;
# - a log on a tuft of grass: the grass burns away and the log doesn't light.
world skill-grounds.toml
as player
luck average

go to the hearth
go to grass
gather grass x6
go to twigs
gather twigs x8
go to sticks
gather sticks x8
go to deadwood
gather deadwood
go to stones
gather stones x20

# Laid first, then lit, at the hearth.
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring
put grass in ring
repeat 3
  put twig in ring
end
put stick in ring
put stick in ring
rub stick against stick into ring
wait 5 min
look
expect said stick of wood and ash
expect said burning

# A log on a tuft alone, at the knapping ground: the tuft burns away, and
# the log won't light.
go to the knapping ground
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring
put grass in ring
put log in ring
rub stick against stick into ring
wait 5 min
look
expect said wood log (3 kg
expect not said log of wood

# An ember made in one ring and tipped into tinder in another, at the
# clearing in the middle. A whole tuft is too much tinder for it.
walk to 0 0
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring
put grass in ring
put twig in ring
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring
rub stick against stick into smallest ring for 75 s
tip dust into largest ring
wait until grass is burning
expect said the grass still isn't burning
# Teased fine, it catches from a fresh ember.
divide grass
divide smallest grass
put smallest grass in largest ring
rub stick against stick into smallest ring for 75 s
tip wood and ash dust into largest ring
wait until grass is burning
expect said the grass is burning
wait until twig is burning
expect said the twig is burning

# An open fire, on bare ground in the woodland: grass at your feet, rubbed
# over, then twigs and sticks as each catches.
go to the woodland
drop grass
rub stick against stick
expect said smouldering
look
expect said lump of dry grass (5 g, 500 K, burning
expect said burning
drop twig
drop twig
wait until twig is burning
expect said the twig is burning
drop twig
drop twig
wait 20
drop stick
drop stick
wait until stick is burning
expect said the stick is burning
expect alive
expect conserved
