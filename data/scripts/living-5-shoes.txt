# Living with the island, stage 5: hide and shoes. The islander's people
# know hide, but not leather, and have no shoes. Hunt a boar and take its
# hide; dry it hard by a fire, where it becomes something the islander has no
# word for, and name it. Cut it, twist cord, and put them together: something
# new, named, and worn on the feet. The mountain's stony paths wear the
# shoes instead of the feet, until they wear through; another pair is made
# the same way. A cloak of leather keeps in body heat. See
# docs/challenges/living-with-the-island.md.
world living.toml
as survivor
luck average

include skills/hunt-a-boar.txt
expect said hide
take hide
go stream
repeat 12
  try drink water
end

# Dry the hide by a fire in the ring.
drop fire ring
put grass in ring
rub wood against wood into ring
wait 10
repeat 5
  put grass in ring
end
wait 10
repeat 4
  put smallest wood in ring
end
wait 30
put wood in ring
put hide in ring
wait 30 min
look
expect said lump of stiff dried skin
wait 2 h
take stiff dried skin from ring
call the stiff dried skin leather
expect said you call it leather.
inventory
expect said lump of leather
repeat 12
  try drink water
end

# Cut it: a cloak's worth, and two pairs' worth.
divide leather
divide smallest leather
divide smallest leather

# Twist cord from bush fibre, and put the leather and cord together.
go forest
wait 5 h
gather bushes
divide fibre
work largest fibre into rope
join smallest leather and rope
expect said you've made something new: lump of leather
expect said what do you call it?
call it shoes
expect said "make shoes" will make another
wear shoes on your feet
expect said you wear the shoes of leather
wear largest leather
expect said you put on the lump of leather
inventory
expect said 1.57743 kg, worn
expect said on your feet

# Up the stony paths to the ridge and back: the shoes, not the feet, take
# the wear, and wear through on the way down.
go hillside
inventory
go slopes
expect not said cuts your feet
go ridge
expect not said cuts your feet
go slopes
expect said your shoes of leather, plant fibre and rotting flesh wore through
inventory
expect said worn through
# Worn-through shoes are as good as bare feet.
go hillside
expect said the rough ground cuts your feet

# Another pair, made the same way.
work fibre into rope
take off shoes
drop shoes
make shoes
wear shoes on your feet
expect said you wear the shoes of leather
go forest
go stream
repeat 12
  try drink water
end
sleep
go forest
go hillside
go slopes
expect not said cuts your feet
expect alive
expect conserved
