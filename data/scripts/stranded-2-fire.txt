# Stranded, stage 2: fire from two sticks. Rubbing wears dust off one stick
# and friction heats it until it smoulders. A tuft of grass on the ember
# catches; then feed the fire one size at a time, once the last size has
# caught: more tufts, then twigs, then sticks.
world stranded.toml
as survivor
luck average

# Gather fuel in sizes (tufts of grass, twigs, sticks) and five stones, and
# build a fire ring to hold the fire.
go forest
gather sticks
gather sticks
repeat 3
  gather grass
end
repeat 3
  gather twigs
end
go hillside
repeat 5
  gather stones
end
assemble fire ring
drop fire ring

put grass in ring
# Rub until the tuft catches: rubbing stops by itself when it does.
rub wood against wood into ring
wait 10
look
expect said lump of dry grass and ash
expect said burning
put grass in ring
put grass in ring
wait 10
repeat 3
  put smallest wood in ring
end
wait 30
put wood in ring
put wood in ring
wait 10 min
look
expect said lump of wood and ash
expect said burning
expect alive
expect conserved
